About

Dr. S. Ahmad is an educationist, psychologist, author and mentor. His work brings together modern psychology, Islamic wisdom, education and holistic wellbeing — in books, printable planners and practical tools for everyday life.

EducationistPsychologistAuthorMentor

What I do

Books: titles on decision-making, the power of speech, and relationships — written in plain language, grounded in research and faith, each chapter closing with something you can practise the same day.

Planners: printable daily, weekly and nutrition planners in one calm design family — tools for intention, habits and balance rather than just productivity.

QalamPress: a done-for-you manuscript-to-market service for independent authors, with a specialist edge for Muslim authors and Islamic educators.

Mentoring: one-to-one guidance on decisions, manuscripts, study direction and personal habits.

How this site works

This website is a catalogue and enquiry point. There is no account system and no checkout here: you browse the books, planners and services, add items to the Enquiry Basket, and send a single message — it opens your email app, so you stay in control of what you share. I reply personally with ordering, payment and delivery details.

A note on wellbeing content

Articles and planners touching on psychology, nutrition or homeopathy are general information for education — not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult your own qualified doctor for health concerns.
