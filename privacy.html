Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. What this site collects

Almost nothing. This is a static website with no accounts, no checkout and no tracking profiles. Pages you visit are served directly; we do not run analytics that identify you.

2. Enquiries and forms

The Enquiry Basket, mentoring form and contact form open your own email application with a pre-filled message. What you type stays in your browser until you press send in your email app — nothing is transmitted to or stored on this website. When you email me, I receive only what you chose to send, and I use it solely to reply to your enquiry.

3. Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is used only to send you the newsletter and the free planner download. You can unsubscribe at any time; your address is never sold or shared.

4. Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Embedded content (e.g. fonts loaded from Google Fonts) may be subject to the provider's own privacy practices.

5. Third-party services

Book purchases completed through external retailers (Draft2Digital, Amazon/KDP, Apple Books, Kobo) are governed by those retailers' privacy policies, not this one.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact page.
