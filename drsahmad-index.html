Questions

Before you enquire

How do I buy a book or planner?

Add it to the Enquiry Basket and tap “Send Enquiry”. That opens an email listing your items — I'll reply with the payment link (Draft2Digital / Amazon where available) and delivery files. Planners are delivered as print-ready A4 PDFs; Canva-editable links can be shared on request.

Are the planners editable?

Yes. The Nutrition Planner (6 pages) and Daily Health Planner were built in the same design family and are available as editable Canva designs as well as print-ready PDFs, so you can adjust meals, targets and notes before printing.

Is SocialHub available to download yet?

Not yet on the Play Store. The Android scaffold is built and runs on preview (mock) data. Live Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp connections need platform API keys — join the waitlist and I'll notify you at launch.

Do you offer bulk or signed copies?

Yes for books and planners — mention quantity and purpose in your enquiry (school, study circle, gifts) and I'll quote accordingly.
